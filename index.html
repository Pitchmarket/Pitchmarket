    How PitchMarket works

    A free football trading game: back the clubs you rate, and finish top of the leaderboard.

    Trade clubs

    Everyone starts with 2,000 Fan Coins. Buy shares in clubs from the Market Explorer and sell whenever you like. Fan Coins are free and have no cash value.

    Prices follow real results

    After every real match, each club's price moves on the result and how big an upset it was. Trading on a club pauses from kickoff until its new price is set, and you can make one trade every few seconds.

    Climb the leaderboard

    Your score is your coins plus everything you hold at today's prices. Pick a display name by tapping your coin balance at the top.

    Daily Kickabout

    Four new football games every day at midnight UK time: Scouted (guess the player from clues), Journeyman (guess them from their career), Link-Up (connect two players through club or international teammates) and Ball Knowledge (a four-question quiz). Each win pays 25 Fan Coins.

    Spotlight and charts

    The Spotlight shows the week's biggest price movers; click any of them to see its chart. Every chart is drawn from real price history.

    Watchlist and alerts

    Star a club to follow it, see its last five results and set a price you want to hear about.

    Make it yours

    The palette button at the top switches between 13 looks, from Normal and Dark to Retro and Chalk tactics. Your choice is remembered.

    Choose your display name

    This is how you appear on the leaderboard. Use 2–24 letters, numbers, spaces, dots, dashes or underscores.

    Choose a new password for your account.
